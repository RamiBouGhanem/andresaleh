# Presentation assets

The original real-coach, stock-training, real-coaching and real-physio photos are retained from the supplied project archive. They are sample imagery; replace the coach portrait with Andre’s own approved photograph before a client launch.

Three AI-generated fictional transformation diptychs are labeled as demos, not client testimonials or documented coaching outcomes. Two added images were generated with the built-in image tool and optimized to WebP.

Prompt for the body-recomposition demo: Two equal portrait panels of the same fictional adult male age 32; neutral gray gym wall; dark navy shorts; identical face, stance, camera and natural lighting. Left: average untrained, visibly overweight build. Right: believable lean athletic muscular build. Frame the head to mid-shin; no text, logos or exaggeration; exact center dividing line.

Prompt for the strength demo: Two equal portrait panels of the same fictional adult male age 26; warm gray gym wall; black shorts; identical face, stance, camera and natural lighting. Left: slim beginner with little muscle. Right: stronger athletic chest, shoulders and arms. Frame the head to mid-shin; no text, logos or exaggeration; exact center dividing line.

The WhatsApp vector uses the familiar WhatsApp mark; Instagram uses the established outline camera icon. Both are rendered directly in the contact control. Font licenses are included in public/fonts/.
